# keel as witness, not enforcer — a nightly digest to one human

- Came out of a bedtime-curfew session (2026-08-07, ~23:00). Four walls tried in one
  night: keel's coding-tool gate, the `/etc/hosts` vice-block, `pmset repeat sleep`,
  macOS Screen Time. All four fail on the same hinge — **the owner holds the key**.
  Commitment strength comes from key custody, not from which layer the lock sits at.
- Descending the stack (coding tools → hosts → DNS → network extension) buys *reach*,
  never *commitment*. A DNS actuator would widen coverage to native apps and the phone,
  and still lose to twenty seconds of motivated `sudo` at 00:45.
- Meadows read: building the wall is level 10 (material structure). Repeated pushing
  without effect means the leverage is higher. The accessible move is **level 6,
  information flows** — keel already writes the log and has never told anyone.
  Toxic Release Inventory: mandatory disclosure cut emissions ~40% with no rule change
  and no enforcement at all.
- A DNS actuator would also be a regression toward the shield layer retired
  2026-06-12. Interventions came back on 2026-08-05 as an *interpreter* — rules as
  data, gate as one actuator, scoped by area. An enforcement layer is not the next
  step in that arc; it's a walk back.
- Domain-wise the slot already exists: a host-level blackhole is a `cooldown`
  actuator, not `actuate` (contract 7 is DOM-scoped and can't see a native app).
  `CooldownSpec` already carries `standing` + `out_of_band` release, and
  `AmbientPrimitive = Exclude<PrimitiveSpec, CooldownSpec>` means a tide could never
  arm it. So the constraint is right even if we never build it.
- Sibling but **inverted**: `2026-06-26-keel-logs-to-private-daily-summaries.md` is
  local-only, for himself. This one points outward, to one chosen person. That
  inversion is the whole idea, and it's also the whole risk.

- Questions:
  - The privacy posture says everything stays on-device. Key custody needs a second
    party. Does exporting a *digest* (three nights past the line) rather than the log
    actually honour the contract, or just bend it? First time keel has hit this.
  - Who is the witness? A person, a group, or a paid referee (stickK/Beeminder shape)?
    Different products, different failure modes — a friend defects quietly, a referee
    doesn't.
  - Does disclosure alone carry it, or does it need stakes attached? The BCT
    literature on commitment contract vs. social support is not ambiguous; read it
    before assuming.
  - Is this keel or is it kairos? It touches another human, which nothing in keel does
    yet. Might be the kernel's job.
  - What happens on a night the digest says nothing wrong? Silence has to be cheap or
    the witness stops reading.

Don't shape yet.
