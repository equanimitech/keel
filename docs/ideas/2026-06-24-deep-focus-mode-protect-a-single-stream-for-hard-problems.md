## The frame (not anti-multitasking)

Throughput-multitasking (fire a session, hop to another while it grinds) is real leverage (~1.95x measured) and stays the default. Deep-focus mode is a separate, **opt-in** mode for the subset of work that needs one sustained line of your own thought: hard problems. Do not force single sessions just because.

## Why it is needed (from the 2026-06-24 EDA)

Single-context flow is ~0 today (dwell median = 1 message). The AI gap is filled with browser drift (61h browser vs 0.1h offline). When you would want to think deeply, the browser-tab reflex pulls you out. The mode exists to make the stream survivable.

## Three moves

1. **Single stream.** Stay in one session; the harness discourages the cross-session hop and the browser-tab reflex while the mode is on.
2. **Breath in the gap.** When the agent grinds, default the gap to an ambient breath / embodied micro-break instead of the browser. This subsumes and is validated by [[2026-06-12-ai-wait-gap-wheel]] (that idea is the menu; this is the mode that points the gap at it). Meta-awareness is the top-evidence lever in the research basis; periphery-first; nudge not block; opt-in (high-work-control user).
3. **Capture mid-stream ideas, do not chase.** An off-thread thought goes to a one-keystroke capture queue (`/idea`, `/sidenote`, `/question`; the `intention` skill already auto-captures off-focus thoughts) instead of becoming a session-switch. This is the thing that actually breaks a stream.

## Where it lives

Not either/or. **Supernote** = where the deep thinking happens (analog, offline, the 0.1h that should grow). **The harness** = what protects the stream (suppress the tab reflex, offer the breath, catch the idea). Possibly a keel `heading`-style session mode (sibling of intention).

## Claim discipline

Breath = recovery + noticing (supported). NOT a deep-focus performance booster (not claimed). The mode is opt-in and reserved for hard problems; the default stays throughput-multitasking.
