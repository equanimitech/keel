# Garmin meditation tracking

Track meditation using Yoga activity type (not Breathwork — limited to 15min). Yoga tracks stress, Body Battery, HR, respiration with no time limit. Can rename a copy to 'Meditation'.

Respiration rate is inferred from PPG (±1.5 brpm at rest, worse at low HR), reliable for trends not individual breath counts. HR and stress curves more useful than respiration for seeing when mind settles.
