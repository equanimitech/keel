# AI-gap spinner → passive learning system

While Claude Code works, it shows a small spinner with rotating tips.
At some point I realized I glance at that thing a few hundred times a day, which makes it some of the most valuable screen real estate.
This gave me a brilliant idea.
To turn it into a passive learning system.
My spinner now shows things I actually want to learn:
Russian words, cognitive biases, rare French words, physics, math etc...
It gives me a few seconds of passive exposure, dozens of times a day, in a place my eyes were already going anyway.
It also slightly reduces the annoying instinct to grab my phone every time Claude takes more than 1.7 seconds to respond.
Sharing it below for anyone who wants it.
